Gemstones & Their Meanings

A small drawer of stones from the curio cabinet, and the gentle energy each one is said to carry.

These meanings are offered as traditional folklore and a starting point for reflection, not scientific or medical claims. Hold whichever stone calls to you, and see what it stirs.

Amethyst

Calm · Intuition · Protection

A stone of quiet clarity. Amethyst is said to soothe an anxious mind, deepen intuition, and offer gentle protection during meditation or reflection.

Rose Quartz

Compassion · Self-Love · Healing

Known as the stone of unconditional love, rose quartz is often reached for during times of heartbreak, or when self-compassion feels hard to find.

Clear Quartz

Clarity · Amplification · Focus

Called the master healer, clear quartz is said to clear mental fog and amplify intention, a stone many keep near for focus and clarity of purpose.

Black Tourmaline

Grounding · Protection · Boundaries

A grounding stone, often kept near the door or the body, said to absorb heavy energy and help hold healthy boundaries with the world.

Selenite

Cleansing · Peace · Higher Self

Named for the moon, selenite is said to cleanse both space and spirit, inviting calm and a sense of connection to something higher.

Moonstone

New Beginnings · Feminine Energy · Intuition

Tied to lunar cycles, moonstone is often carried during new beginnings, said to ease transitions and awaken inner intuition.

Citrine

Abundance · Joy · Confidence

Known as the merchant's stone, citrine is associated with abundance and personal confidence, said to carry a warmth that never needs cleansing.

Green Aventurine

Luck · Growth · Opportunity

Often called the stone of opportunity, thought to attract luck and gentle growth, especially when stepping into something new.

Lapis Lazuli

Truth · Wisdom · Expression

A deep blue stone of honest expression, said to support clear communication and the wisdom to speak one's own truth.
