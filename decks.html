Choosing a Deck

Decks are not picked at random. Each week's theme suggests its own company, and most readings use two or three together.

✦

A tarot deck to anchor

One tarot deck carries the structure of the reading, the arc from beginning to resolution. Everything else is drawn around it.

✦

An oracle to soften

Oracle cards add warmth and plain language where tarot can feel formal. They often carry the line you actually remember afterwards.

✦

Matched to the week

Shadow work weeks lean on the Shadow Work Oracle and the Witch's Garden. Angel weeks call in the Whisper Cards. Ocean weeks belong to the tides.
