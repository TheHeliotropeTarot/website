Our Story

Why heliotropes, why tarot, and why this space exists.

Like the heliotrope flower that always turns towards the sun, I believe we are all constantly seeking light, even in our darkest moments. This channel began as a simple wish: to create a warm, honest space where tarot and oracle readings could offer clarity, hope, and the gentle reminder that you are exactly where you need to be.

Tarot, here, is never about prediction or fortune-telling. It's about clarity and inner knowing, a mirror for what you may already sense but haven't yet found the words for. Every reading is delivered honestly, heartfully, and with love, whether you're new to tarot or a seasoned seeker.

A Note on Who I Am

For now, I've chosen to let the readings speak for themselves rather than stepping fully into the spotlight myself. This isn't about hiding, it's about making sure that what resonates with you is the message, not the messenger. As this journey grows and I find my footing, I look forward to sharing more of myself with this community. For now, thank you for trusting the cards, and trusting me, even without a face attached to the voice.
